# IT Security Policy

## Password Requirements

All employees must use passwords containing at least 12 characters, including uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and special characters. Passwords must not be shared with colleagues or reused across personal and company accounts.

## Multi-Factor Authentication

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all company accounts, including email, cloud services, and internal applications. Employees must use the company-approved authenticator application. MFA requests that the employee did not initiate must be rejected and reported to IT immediately.

## Company Devices

Employees must use company-issued laptops and devices for business activities. Devices must be protected with a password or biometric authentication and must have the company-approved antivirus software installed. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to IT within one hour of discovery.

## Software Installation

Employees may only install software approved by the IT department. Installing unlicensed, pirated, or unauthorized software on company devices is prohibited. Employees who need additional software must submit a request through the IT support portal.

## Security Incidents

Employees must immediately report suspected security incidents to the IT security team through security@nexora.example. Examples include phishing emails, malware warnings, unauthorized account access, and accidental exposure of company information. Employees must not attempt to investigate or resolve serious incidents independently.

## Data Protection

Company confidential information must only be stored in approved company storage systems. Employees must not transfer confidential company files to personal email accounts, personal cloud storage, or unauthorized external devices. Access to sensitive information must only be granted to employees who need it for their work.
