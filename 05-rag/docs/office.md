# Office and Workplace Policy

## Office Access

Employees may access the office between 07:00 and 20:00 on weekdays using their assigned access cards. Access cards must not be shared with other employees or visitors. Lost access cards must be reported to the office administration team immediately.

## Meeting Room Reservations

Meeting rooms must be reserved through the company's online booking calendar. Employees may reserve rooms for a maximum of two hours per booking. Reservations should be cancelled if the room is no longer needed so other employees can use it.

## Visitors and Guests

All visitors must be registered at reception upon arrival. Employees are responsible for accompanying their visitors throughout their stay. Visitors must not enter restricted office areas without explicit authorization from the relevant department.

## Office Equipment

Shared office equipment, including printers, monitors, and presentation equipment, must be used responsibly. Employees must report damaged or malfunctioning equipment to the office administration team. Company equipment must not be removed from the premises without prior approval.

## Workplace Safety

Employees must keep emergency exits and corridors clear at all times. Any accident, injury, or unsafe workplace condition must be reported to the office administration team immediately. Employees must familiarize themselves with the building's evacuation procedures.

## Parking and Bicycle Storage

Employees may use the designated bicycle storage area free of charge. Car parking spaces are limited and must be reserved through office administration at least one working day in advance. Unauthorized parking in reserved spaces is prohibited.
