# Finance and Expenses Policy

## Expense Reimbursement

Employees may request reimbursement for reasonable business-related expenses. All reimbursement requests must be submitted through the company expense portal within 30 calendar days of the purchase. Original receipts or valid digital receipts must be attached to every request.

## Meal Expenses

Employees travelling for business may claim meal expenses up to €25 per day. Alcoholic beverages are not reimbursable. Meal expenses must be supported by receipts and submitted through the expense portal.

## Business Travel

All business trips must be approved by the employee's direct manager before any bookings are made. Employees should choose reasonably priced transportation and accommodation options. Train travel is preferred for journeys under 500 kilometres when practical.

## Equipment Purchases

Employees must obtain approval from their department manager before purchasing equipment costing more than €150. All equipment purchased with company funds remains the property of Nexora Technologies and must be registered with the IT department when applicable.

## Payment Processing

Approved reimbursement requests are normally processed within 10 business days. Payments are made through the employee's registered payroll bank account. Employees should contact the finance department if a payment has not arrived within this period.

## Corporate Cards

Corporate cards are issued only to employees whose roles require regular business purchases. Cards may only be used for authorized business expenses. Personal purchases using corporate cards are strictly prohibited. Lost or stolen corporate cards must be reported to the finance department immediately.
