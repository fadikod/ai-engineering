# Human Resources Policy

## Working Hours

Employees at Nexora Technologies work 40 hours per week,
Monday through Friday. Standard working hours are from
09:00 to 17:30, including a 30-minute unpaid lunch break.

## Sick Leave

Employees who are unable to work due to illness must
notify their direct team lead before 09:00 on the first
day of their absence. Notification must be sent through
email or the company's internal messaging system.
If the absence continues beyond three consecutive
working days, the employee must contact HR.

## Annual Leave

Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave
per calendar year. Employees must submit leave requests
through the HR portal at least two weeks before the
requested start date. All leave requests require
manager approval.

## Remote Work

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week.
Remote work requires prior approval from their direct
manager. Employees must remain reachable during standard
working hours, regardless of their work location.

## Parental Leave

Employees who become parents may request parental leave
through the HR department. The duration and payment
conditions depend on applicable local employment
regulations and the employee's contract. Employees should
contact HR at least four weeks before their planned leave
whenever possible.

## Probation Period

New employees have a probation period of two months,
unless their employment contract specifies otherwise.
During this period, both the employee and Nexora
Technologies may evaluate whether the employment
arrangement is suitable. Any termination must comply
with applicable employment law.
